How should the valley look?

    
This is the real valley with the game's renderer, in two looks: Today is what the game draws now, and Proposed is the art plan's look: whiter snow, blue shade, softer light, haze in the distance and a light colour grade.

    
Flip between them, or hold C, at the fixed views. Move the sliders to tune the proposed look. Walk from any view with WASD.

    
Only the midday light exists for now. Dawn, dusk and night come with the day cycle.
