Take a winter walk

    
This is the real valley with the walking, camera and footstep code the game will use. Walk on fresh powder, the packed road and the lake ice and listen to how each one sounds. Now and then a short felt-piano phrase plays.

    
WASD to walk, Shift to jog, Space to hop. Click or drag to look around, and use the wheel to zoom. A gamepad works too.

    
Change anything in the panel while you walk, then copy your settings to me.
