## Part 4 — Upgrades and Progression

### 4.1 Progression model
- **Five tracks progress in parallel**, so there's always a next thing:
  1. **Tools:** efficiency and feel.
  2. **Hauling and truck:** capacity and access.
  3. **Warmth:** time outdoors and comfort.
  4. **Home and shed:** storage, seasoning and cozy.
  5. **People and places:** arcs, areas and tiers.
- **Time conversion:** a typical player sleeps through most nights, so **play hours ≈ in-game Day × 0.47**. Hour 10 is about Day 21; Lantern Night (Day 28) is about hour 13.
- **Gates** (area access) are spaced about 3 hours apart. **Big feel upgrades** (maul, truck, plow, chains, drying loft) are spaced so one lands roughly every 1.5–2 hours.
- **Totals:**
  - All functional upgrades cost ≈ **9,300c**.
  - Cosmetics and decor add ≈ **2,000c**.
  - The main path through all gates costs ≈ **2,500c**, reached around hour 10.
  - Completion of everything is around 24–26 h.
