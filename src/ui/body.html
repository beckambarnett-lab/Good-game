How to play

  1. Pick a unit from the tray at the bottom and click or drag on the blue zone to place it. Every unit costs gold.
  2. Remove units with right-click, or switch on the Eraser and tap them.
  3. Press Start and watch. Nobody listens to orders once the fighting begins.
  4. During battle: click a unit to ride along with it. Pause, slow-mo and 2× are top right.
  5. Press Possess (or P) while following one of your units to fight as it. Aim with the mouse, click to attack. The crosshair snaps to whatever you point at.
