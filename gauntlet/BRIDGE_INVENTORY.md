## Layout (game coordinates)
- **Shop and deck:** the shop front is at z = 0 (x -2.7..2.7). The footbridge deck is at y 3.6, x -6.4..-4.0, running from z 1.0 (the shop end, beside the shop's 2F balcony, in front of the soba shop) to z 13.9 (the far end, at the konbini).
- **Far stairs:** they come down toward +x along the far sidewalk (z 12.65..13.9, from x -4 at the top to x 1 at the bottom). In B-street and A-cross these are the foreground stairs, and in A-balcony they're the stairs on the left.
- **Near stairs:** they come down toward -x along the shop-side kerb (x -6.4 at the top to x -11.4 at the bottom), into the Kaiun site. These are the distant stairs under the bridge in B-street and A-cross, and the stairs beside 開運 KAIUN in A-konbini.

## Never changes (identical at every stage)
- **The konbini ほしマート** under the bridge's far end: white fascia with teal and orange stripes and a yellow star logo, a bright interior with shelves, and an umbrella stand by the door. (In the game today it's "AMA MART" with rainbow stripes, which needs fixing.)
- **The 開運 KAIUN karaoke parlor** on the shop side, west of the near stairs: a pink-lit glass front with 開運 KAIUN in pink neon (A-konbini left).
- **The street itself:**
  - Concrete apartment blocks with AC units, the cyan neon strip on the building opposite, and the skyline towers with lit windows.
  - Overhead wires, the tall street lamps (cool white), and the GARCADE neon at the right of B-street and A-cross.
- **The road:** clean, glossy wet black asphalt with crisp mirror-like reflections (no speckle noise, no wavy distortion), white lane markings, grey paving sidewalks with yellow tactile strips.
- **Rain:** heavy, visible streaks; splashes on the deck and road.
