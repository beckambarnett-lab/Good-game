# Bridge references

Target images for the pedestrian bridge (the 8 Line footbridge outside the shop), used as a constant reference while iterating on it.

**before** = the bridge as the game starts (prologue, unrestored).
**after** = the finished bridge terrace (late game, fully restored).

| File | View | What to match |
|---|---|---|
| `before-01-street-low-stairs.png` | Street level, low, beside the stairs, looking along the bridge to the shop | Rusted, graffitied steel panel walls with peeling paint and rust streaks. A steep steel stair with yellow caution tape. A second stair down at the far end by the konbini (ほしマート). Thin cylindrical support columns. Litter and cans in the gutter. |
| `before-02-balcony-overhead.png` | From the shop's 2nd-floor balcony, looking down across the bridge | The deck spans the whole street. A 立入禁止 (no entry) sign and a striped barrier at the shop end, with a cone and a dead potted plant. A single fluorescent tube under the deck. A cyan neon strip on the building opposite. |
| `after-01-street-low-stairs.png` | Same angle as before-01 | Glazed canopy on dark timber posts, with string lights in warm and coloured bulbs. Wisteria and ivy spill over the side, droplet lanterns, a cyan underglow strip under the deck edge, a neon teacup sign. The stair treads have warm LED nosing. A テラス席 ↑ A-board at the stair foot. |
| `after-02-balcony-to-terrace.png` | Same angle as before-02 | Tables under the canopy, copper patio heaters, white droplet lanterns. Wisteria planters along the deck rail, and red pots along the balcony rail. |
| `after-03-street-head-on.png` | Head-on down the street, elevated, from the crosswalk | A 雫テラス timber sign on the bridge fascia, centred, with a cyan strip below it. Symmetrical composition. Lamps lining both sidewalks. |
| `after-04-terrace-deck.png` | Standing on the deck | Round café tables and red-cushion stools. Copper heaters with glowing columns. Timber planter walls with wisteria and bamboo. A glass roof with rain running off it, string lights, and a pink 雫テラス neon. A 関係者以外立入禁止 plaque. |
| `after-05-street-under-bridge.png` | Street level from the konbini side, under the bridge | The fascia shows the 雫テラス sign plus a neon teacup. A concrete column, stair lanterns, a bicycle and potted plants at the stair foot. |
